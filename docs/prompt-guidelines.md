# Prompt Guidelines

This document provides guidelines for creating effective prompts for interacting with the HR Attrition Analysis System.

## General Principles

*   **Be specific:** The more specific your prompt, the more accurate the response will be.
*   **Provide context:** Include relevant information, such as employee IDs, date ranges, or department names.
*   **Use natural language:** You can ask questions in plain English.

## Examples

*   "Show me the attrition predictions for the sales department."
*   "Generate a report on employee feedback for the last quarter."
*   "What are the top reasons for attrition?"
